Birds: A Fascinating Insight into the Avian World

Birds are a diverse group of vertebrate animals known for their feathers, toothless beaked jaws, and the laying of hard-shelled eggs. They are warm-blooded, live worldwide, and range in size from the 5.5 cm (2.2 in) bee hummingbird to the 2.8 m (9 ft 2 in) common ostrich. There are over 11,000 living species of birds, classified in 44 orders. More than half of the world's bird species are passerine or "perching" birds. Nearly all birds have wings, which are modified forelimbs, giving them the ability to fly. Some birds, like parrots, can mimic human speech and other sounds. Birds play an essential role in ecosystems by pollinating flowers and controlling insect populations. Watching birds can be a peaceful and enjoyable hobby, whether in a backyard or a park. Overall, birds contribute to the beauty and diversity of our natural world.
